Persona Taboo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

Persona Taboo (“the App”, “the Game”) is a party guessing game published by EnkiGames (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the App handles, how it is used, and the third-party services involved. By using the App you agree to this policy.

Information you actively provide

The App only stores information when you choose to submit it:

  • Display name. A name you type to join a game. It is shown to other players in the same game and on the scoreboard. It is not required to be your real name.
  • Feedback. If you use the optional “Send feedback” box, we store the text you write, along with the score for that session and the date and time. We do not store your display name with it, and we do not attach any device identifier or account to it. Feedback is anonymous.
  • Difficult-word reports. If you flag words as “difficult” at the end of a game, we store which words you flagged, along with the score, game language, and the date and time — never your display name.

Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not require you to create an account or provide an email address, phone number, or password.
  • We do not collect your real name, contacts, photos, location, or files.
  • Our own game servers do not log device identifiers alongside your feedback.
  • We never write your display name to a file or database — it lives only in server memory for the length of a game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process device information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, and app-interaction data. This processing is governed by Google’s policies, not ours:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

Ads appear as a full-screen ad on the game-over screen, not during play. We do not control the specific ads shown or the data Google collects to serve them.

How we use the information

  • To run the game (show your display name and score to other players in your session, for the length of that game).
  • To read your feedback and improve the game.
  • To identify words players find too hard and adjust the word library.

We do not sell your information. We do not use it for profiling beyond the advertising described above.

Data retention

Live game state (display names, scores) exists only in server memory during a game and is discarded when the game ends or the server restarts — it is never written to a file or a database. Feedback and difficult-word reports are appended to simple log files, without any name attached, and kept only for as long as they are useful for improving the game.

Children

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Because it displays third-party ads, we do not knowingly target or collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the feedback box, contact us and we will remove it.

Your choices

  • Providing a display name, feedback, and difficult-word flags is entirely optional (a name is needed only to join a game).
  • You can reset your device’s advertising identifier or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings.
  • Deletion. Because we store nothing that identifies you, there is normally nothing to delete. If you want a specific piece of feedback you sent removed, email enkigames.info@gmail.com with roughly when you sent it and what it said, and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects the latest version.
