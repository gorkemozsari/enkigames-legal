Angry Balls — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026

Angry Balls (“the App”, “the Game”) is a mobile game published by EnkiGames (“we”, “us”). We respect your privacy. This policy explains, simply, what information the App handles — and the short version is: we do not collect any personal information from you. By using the App you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

None. Angry Balls does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Specifically, the App:

  • does not require you to create an account or sign in;
  • does not ask for your name, email, phone number, or any contact details;
  • does not show advertising;
  • does not use analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting services;
  • does not collect your location, contacts, photos, or files.

Data stored on your device

Any game data — such as your progress, high scores, or settings — is stored only on your own device. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else, and it is removed if you uninstall the App.

Third-party services

Angry Balls does not integrate any third-party advertising, analytics, or social SDKs. No third parties receive data from the App.

The App is distributed through the Google Play Store. Any data Google collects as the app store (for example, download statistics) is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy, not by us.

Children

Angry Balls is suitable for a general audience. Because the App collects no personal information and shows no ads, it does not knowingly gather data from children or anyone else.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects the latest version.
